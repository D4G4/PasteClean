# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 15, 2026

## Overview

PasteClean is a local-only email formatting tool. It processes your email content entirely on your device. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

## Data Collection

PasteClean does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or tracking pixels.
- **No network requests.** The app operates entirely offline. It does not connect to any server, API, or cloud service.
- **No account required.** There is no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind.
- **No data leaves your device.** All email content you write or paste is processed locally on your iPhone. The sanitized output is placed on your device's clipboard — it is never uploaded anywhere.

## What the App Does

PasteClean helps you compose emails that look correct when pasted into Gmail. It:

1. Provides a rich text editor for composing email content.
2. Sanitizes the HTML to remove styles that Gmail would render incorrectly (e.g., dark-mode colors that become invisible on a white background).
3. Copies the sanitized HTML to your device's clipboard so you can paste it into Gmail.

All of this happens locally on your device.

## Third-Party Services

PasteClean does not integrate with any third-party services. When you tap "Open Gmail," the app uses iOS's standard URL scheme to open the Gmail app — no data is passed through this action beyond the app launch request.

## Children's Privacy

PasteClean does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL. Since PasteClean collects no data, we do not anticipate material changes.
